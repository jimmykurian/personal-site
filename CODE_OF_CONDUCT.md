# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This is a personal project, but anyone who takes part in it, by reporting an
issue, reviewing a change or writing in about the site, should be treated with
respect, whatever their background or experience.

## Our standards

**Expected behavior:**

- Being kind, patient and professional
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Respecting other people's privacy

**Unacceptable behavior:**

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission, including
  details about the clients, employers and people mentioned on the site
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Privacy on this project

- Don't add or ask for real client names, personal contact details, or
  anything the [content rules](CONTRIBUTING.md#content-rules) keep off the site.
- Report a suspected leak of private information privately, as described in
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not in public.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to this repository, its pull requests and reviews,
and correspondence about the site, including email sent to
<hello@jimmykurian.me>.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to <hello@jimmykurian.me>. Every report will be
reviewed promptly and fairly, and kept confidential.

I may remove, edit or reject contributions and comments that don't follow this
Code of Conduct, and may block anyone who repeatedly or seriously breaks it.
